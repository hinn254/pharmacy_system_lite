Build increments and exit gates

E0 — Foundation and baseline proof

Modules: M00

Posting kernel, authorization contracts, model migration inventory, protected audit and source-event idempotency; baseline existing clinical/insurance flows.

Gate: Duplicate/tenant/transaction rollback tests pass before money/stock changes.

E1 — Branch-ready foundation

Modules: M01, M02, M03, M26, M36

Branches/stores/nested locations/access joins; default branch/store migration; chart/posting mappings and sequences; safe gateway configuration. M26/M36 continue later with adapters/reports.

Gate: Two-branch scope and opening balances reconcile; no public secrets or cross-branch mutation.

E2 — Catalog, units, tax and inventory engine

Modules: M04, M05, M06, M07, M08, M11

Master data, product behavior, costs/lots/positions, reservations, FEFO, import preview and supplier terms.

Gate: Pack/loose-unit/free-quantity/cost snapshots and concurrent last-unit allocation prove correct.

E3 — Physical stock operations and procurement

Modules: M09, M10, M12, M13

Counts, approved adjustments, transfer transit/partial receipt, POs/GRNs/direct receiving.

Gate: Branches and transfers are included in the first releasable pilot; 20 dispatch/18 receive/2 unresolved preserves total.

E4 — Commercial operations and finance

Modules: M14, M15, M16, M17, M18, M19, M20, M25, M26, M27, M36, M39

Supplier debt/returns, customer credit, POS/split tender, registers, callback adapters, partial refunds, expenses, reconciliation and statements.

Gate: Financial posting graph balances end-to-end; failed payment cannot release paid checkout; full daily close proves all sources.

E5 — Customer retention and pharmacy depth

Modules: M21, M22, M23, M24, M37

Quotations, delivery print, loyalty/tender instruments, clinical preservation and reminders.

Gate: Parity tender/quote/loyalty/message workflows usable; no medicine returned to sellable stock without review.

E6 — Complete the back-office parity scope

Modules: M28, M29, M30, M31, M32

Native employee/payroll/advances/disbursement statements, ticketing and tools. These are full-parity deliverables, not permanently optional because retail pilot shipped.

Gate: Payroll rules independently validated; employee-period snapshots, statements and no duplicate payments.

E7 — Owner visibility, exports and parity certification

Modules: M33, M34

All observed report families and filters, owner dashboard, large exports, production pilot, restore/import drills and screen-by-screen parity signoff.

Gate: Every observed screen maps to implemented workflow with field/flow/schema/test evidence; no schema-only feature counted complete.

E8 — Surpass the observed baseline

Modules: M35, M38

Offline selling, expiry redistribution/reorder suggestions, stronger clinical review, richer reconciliation and measured UX improvements. Some hardening is mandatory earlier.

Gate: Differentiation claims only after reproducible tests and pharmacy pilot feedback.
