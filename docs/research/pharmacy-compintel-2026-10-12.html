Pharmacy feature gaps

PharmaPOS currently shows broader retail and back-office coverage. Our strongest existing foundation is pharmacy workflow: consultations, pharmacist/cashier/dispensing queues, FEFO allocation, prescription attachments and labels, insurance invoicing, targeted discounts and operational analytics. The first release should add branch/store stock control and close payment, stock and accounting correctness gaps before chasing every module.

Our release readiness findings

M-Pesa confirmation is a release blocker

The callback handler sets MpesaTransaction to COMPLETED when CheckoutRequestID is present, without checking ResultCode, extracting the receipt or updating the linked Payment. A failure can therefore be recorded as completed.

Inspect source

Tenant isolation does not provide branch operations

Organization scopes data today; the schema has no Branch, Store, Location, Transfer or batch-location balance model. A batch is unique by drug and batch number, so adding only branchId to the current batch table will not model shared lots across locations adequately.

Inspect source

Historical profit can change after a cost edit

Analytics computes COGS from SaleLineBatch quantities joined to current DrugBatch.unitCost or Drug.costLast. Snapshot allocated unit cost at sale time before extending branch margin reports. Revenue also comes from Sale.total, which includes tax; define net-sales reporting explicitly.

Inspect source

Tax classes need a real line-level model

Sales use an organization rate and sale-wide tax toggle. There are no per-product tax classes or effective rate records in the current schema. Preserve exempt versus zero-rated semantics instead of representing everything as tax off.

Inspect source

Offline and marketing models are groundwork

OfflineSyncQueue and PromoCampaign exist, but no operational use was found in app/lib/components. The service worker caches GET responses and skips API requests; that is not offline checkout synchronization.

Inspect source

Void and return enums are not completed workflows

SaleStatus.VOIDED and stock RETURN/VOID_RESTOCK enum values exist, but no complete sale-void or partial-refund workflow was found in the reviewed actions and routes.

Inspect source

Observations to investigate

Accounting discrepancy

The trial balance visibly warned that debits and credits differ by KES 11,349. The balance sheet showed assets of 295,570 versus liabilities plus equity of 284,221, the same difference. Demo data and workflow history may explain it; this does not establish a universal product defect.

Observed screen

Stock valuation needs an explicit basis

Dashboard stock valuation was 85,960; stock registry cost valuation was 74,450; balance-sheet inventory valuation was 40,510. Scope, date or costing choices may differ; establish one traceable valuation policy in our system.

Observed screen

Possible double-counting in cash summary

Business Snapshot showed collected 4,150 plus other income 4,100 and net cash position 8,250; the other-income category was Sales. This suggests duplication to investigate, not proof of the underlying cause.

Observed screen

Loyalty consistency needs verification

Customer list showed walk-in points while redemption summary showed zero and no customer records. Eligibility/filter scope could explain it. Our ledger and redemption views should reconcile under the same scope.

Observed screen

Claims exceed inspected workflow evidence

About describes prescription management, refill SMS, encryption and legal compliance. Equivalent clinical/refill screens were not found in the inspected navigation, and no security/compliance claim was independently validated. Generic school-fees wording appeared in M-Pesa settings.

Potential schemas and relationships

Proposed model fields, cardinalities, constraints and migration steps are captured in the schema blueprint. These are inferred workflow patterns and recommended designs, not recovered PharmaPOS tables.

Branches and stock

Organization → Branch → Store → Location. Separate DrugBatch lot identity from location balances, reservations and immutable receipt cost layers. Transfers retain batch and cost through dispatch and partial receipts.

Purchasing and settlements

Purchase-order lines, receipt lines and supplier bill lines connect through allocations. Separate physical receiving from liability creation and payment. Keep existing supplier payment allocation models.

Patient, payer and finance

Keep clinical Patient identity separate from financial CustomerAccount. Sales connect to batch allocations, returns, credits and refunds. Receipts allocate across invoices; balanced journals and verified webhook events preserve traceability.

The blueprint includes relationship diagrams, tenant-consistent foreign keys, posting constraints, default-branch migration and compatibility notes. The operational Prisma schema is unchanged.

Guidance and release sequence

P0

Release 1 — Branch operations and launch correctness

Build branch/store/location scope and staff permissions first; migrate the current organization to one default branch/store. Add batch-location balances, reservations and safe transfer dispatch/receipt. Then complete units, tax classes, immutable costs, payment confirmation, cash registers, returns, direct receiving and POs/GRNs. Reconcile all totals and validate the current eTIMS integration requirements before release.

P1

Release 2 — Owner control and workflow depth

Add customer credit and statements, procurement matching, supplier aging, a balanced accounting ledger and statements, exports, payment-channel recording, reminder delivery, demand-based replenishment and operational exception queues. Ship offline checkout only after duplicate prevention and inventory conflict handling are proven.

P2

Release 3 — Retention and optional back office

Add quotations, loyalty, corporate billing refinements, catalog media and hierarchy, payroll and internal ticketing. Payroll can be an integration instead of a native engine if pharmacy owners prefer an existing accountant/payroll tool.

Architecture implications

Proposed dependency order: Organization → Branch → Store → Location; shared Drug and DrugBatch lot identity → BatchLocationBalance and StockReservation → Transfer/TransferLine/TransferReceipt → PurchaseOrder/GRN/SupplierInvoiceLine → SaleReturn/CreditNote and customer accounts → Journal/JournalLine. Every monetary/stock document needs organization, branch, actor, timestamp, source reference, status, immutable snapshots and an idempotency key. Existing data should migrate to a default branch and store, with totals reconciled before enabling more branches. Separate permissions for clinical records, financial records and branch inventory. Store immutable movement deltas rather than relying on editable quantity fields for historical reconstruction.

Release proof scenarios

Branch and transfer

Receive 100 tablets of batch B1 at Branch A. Dispatch 20 to Branch B. Until receipt, A available is 80 and B sellable is 0; transit is 20. Receive 18 and retain a discrepancy of 2. Replaying receipt changes nothing. Organization stock still totals 100 until an authorized discrepancy write-off.

Units and expiry

Receive one pack of 100, sell six units from the earliest usable batch, and return two approved units to quarantine. Available sellable quantity is 94 until review; expiry and cost remain attached to the lot.

Payments

A failed M-Pesa callback cannot complete payment or clear debt; successful callbacks reconcile receipt and amount. Replaying the same callback produces no additional payment or accounting entry.

Cost and margin

Sell at net price 100 from a batch costing 60. Updating that batch to cost 70 later leaves the earlier gross profit at 40. Tax and returned quantities are accounted for separately.

Ledger and refunds

Every posted document creates balanced journals. A partial approved return credits only eligible quantities and reverses the appropriate revenue, tax, cost and payment/receivable effects.

Permissions

A staff member assigned only to Branch A cannot view, modify or export Branch B records through direct URLs, server actions or report queries.

Positioning: what to say, clarify and prove

Say: We are building pharmacy workflow and branch inventory control around traceable payments, safe dispensing and reconciled owner reports. This is proposed positioning, not a claim of already deployed superiority.

Clarify: Transfer approvals, loose-unit sales, credit policies, preferred accounting/payroll tools, and insurer/fiscal integrations requiring live proof.

Prove: Two-branch transfers, failed/replayed payment callbacks, partial returns, historical margin stability and a balanced daily close.

What not to overclaim

Visible modules are not execution proof. Unobserved clinical, offline or eTIMS workflows are unknown. Our repository presence is not production readiness. About-page security/legal claims are not independently verified. Kenyan fiscal, privacy and payroll requirements need current official validation before implementation.

Sources, coverage and limitations

Primary sources: the supplied PharmaPOS account and this repository. Knowledge & Files was satisfied by local code. CRM, messaging, meeting transcripts and sales intelligence were not relevant. No credentials or raw customer records are embedded.

All accessible module menu groups were reviewed: POS, sales, inventory, purchases, customers, suppliers, expenses, payroll, accounting, reports, users, SMS/email, settings, About and ticketing. 90 distinct menu-linked pages plus dashboard were inspected. Detail flows requiring new records were left untested.

Confidence: High for cited source-level gaps; moderate for competitor operational breadth; unverified for live integration, compliance, security and clinical claims. Our app was not launched or transaction-tested. No application code changed.
